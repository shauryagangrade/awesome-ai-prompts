# Research

Prompts for research workflows that verify rather than guess: finding and
reading primary sources, checking claims, comparing options, designing
experiments, and turning a literature search into real, analyzed datasets.

Prompts carrying the light-blue badge are the heavyweight
[spec](../README.md#research) prompts: multi-section, with
hard constraints and required verification.

Back to the [main index](../README.md#research) or browse
[every prompt on one page](../website/index.html).

## Prompts

- [literature-review-prompt.md](literature-review-prompt.md) - survey a topic through primary sources: scope, find, verify, synthesize, and report gaps.
- [source-verification-prompt.md](source-verification-prompt.md) - verify a claim end-to-end: split it, trace primaries, rate each part, and state confidence.
- [technical-research-prompt.md](technical-research-prompt.md) <img src="../docs/media/spec-badge.svg" alt="spec" style="vertical-align:-3px"> - deep technical investigation: fixed criteria, reproduced evidence, and a cited recommendation.
- [competitive-analysis-prompt.md](competitive-analysis-prompt.md) - compare competing tools with version-pinned primary evidence and a decision matrix.
- [experiment-design-prompt.md](experiment-design-prompt.md) - turn a question into a reproducible experiment with a falsifiable hypothesis and honest reporting.
- [dataset-discovery-prompt.md](dataset-discovery-prompt.md) <img src="../docs/media/spec-badge.svg" alt="spec" style="vertical-align:-3px"> - mine research papers for fitting datasets, download the actual files, and write a no-fluff `.md` analysis of each.
